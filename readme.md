# 📊 Superstore Sales Analysis (Top-Down Approach)

## 📌 Project Overview
This Power BI project presents a comprehensive analysis of Superstore sales with a **top-down analytical approach**, focusing on shipping costs, customer-level behavior, and operational efficiency.

The dashboard helps uncover patterns in shipping expenses, customer contributions, and delivery methods to support better business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze **shipping cost distribution** across order priorities
- Identify **high-cost customers** and outliers
- Evaluate **shipping modes efficiency**
- Provide insights to optimize logistics and profitability

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Power Query
- Data Visualization Techniques

---

## 📂 Dataset
- Superstore dataset (sample business data)
- Includes:
  - Customer Name
  - Order Date
  - Product Name
  - Profit
  - Shipping Cost
  - Ship Mode
  - Order Priority

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Shipping Cost by Order Priority
- **Low priority orders (22K)** and **Critical orders (21K)** contribute the highest shipping costs
- Medium and Not Specified categories show relatively lower costs (~13K)
- Indicates potential inefficiency in low-priority shipment handling

---

### 2. Customer-Level Shipping Cost Analysis
- Most customers fall within a **low shipping cost range**
- A few customers show **significantly higher costs (~7K–8K)** indicating outliers
- These high-cost customers may require targeted cost optimization strategies

---

### 3. Shipping Mode Analysis
- **Regular Air** dominates with **44.65K (~100%)**
- **Delivery Truck** contributes **34K**
- **Express Air** is the least used (~4.99K, ~11.2%)

👉 Insight:
- Heavy reliance on Regular Air suggests potential for cost optimization by shifting to lower-cost shipping methods where feasible

---

### 4. Profit vs Shipping Cost (Tabular Analysis)
- The table provides detailed transaction-level insights
- Total Profit: **~2.81M**
- Total Shipping Cost: **~83.6K**
- Some products show relatively higher shipping cost compared to profit

---

## 🧠 Business Recommendations

- ✅ Optimize shipping strategy for **low-priority orders**
- ✅ Reduce dependency on **Regular Air shipping**
- ✅ Identify and negotiate with **high-cost customers**
- ✅ Improve profitability by aligning shipping cost with product margins
